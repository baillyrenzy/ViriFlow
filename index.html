Understanding the basics

The three body systems ViriFlow™ is formulated for

The prostate

A walnut-sized gland that is part of the male reproductive system. It sits just below the bladder and surrounds the urethra, the tube that carries urine out of the body.

Because of this position, changes in the prostate over the years can affect how comfortable urination feels. Regular checkups with a doctor are the best way to keep track of prostate health.

The bladder

A flexible, muscular organ that stores urine until you are ready to release it. A healthy bladder relaxes as it fills and contracts when it is time to empty.

Hydration, fiber, a healthy weight, pelvic floor strength and limiting bladder irritants such as too much caffeine all play a part in everyday bladder comfort.

The urinary system

The kidneys filter your blood, the ureters carry urine to the bladder and the urethra completes the path. When every part works smoothly, you hardly notice it.

Staying hydrated and eating a balanced diet helps this system do its job day after day.

The formula

A closer look at every ingredient

The descriptions below explain why each ingredient is popular in wellness nutrition. They describe traditional and general use and are not claims that any single ingredient treats a condition. Check your bottle's supplement facts panel for exact amounts.*

S

Saw palmetto

Prostate and urinary wellness

Saw palmetto is the berry of a small palm tree that grows in the southeastern United States. It has been part of traditional nutrition for a very long time, and today it is one of the most recognized ingredients in men's health supplements.

It is commonly included in formulas that aim to support normal prostate function and urinary comfort. In ViriFlow™, it forms the foundation of the prostate-support side of the blend.*

P

Pomegranate

Antioxidant-rich fruit

Pomegranate is a deep-red fruit known for its natural polyphenols, including compounds called punicalagins. These plant compounds act as antioxidants, which help the body manage everyday oxidative stress.

Pomegranate has been enjoyed for thousands of years across the Mediterranean and Asia, and it is a popular addition to supplements designed for overall wellness and healthy aging.*

Sh

Shilajit

Mineral-rich Ayurvedic resin

Shilajit is a sticky, dark resin that forms slowly in mountain rock, especially in the Himalayas. It contains fulvic acid and a wide range of trace minerals.

In Ayurveda, shilajit has long been valued as a rejuvenating substance that supports energy and vitality. In ViriFlow™, it adds mineral richness and a traditional, whole-body wellness dimension to the formula.*

K

Kelp

Sea vegetable and trace minerals

Kelp is a large brown seaweed that grows in cool coastal waters. It is a natural source of iodine and other trace minerals, and it has been part of coastal diets for centuries.

Kelp is included in the formula as a nutrient-dense marine ingredient that supports general wellness and healthy metabolism.* If you have a thyroid condition, ask your healthcare professional about iodine before using any kelp-containing product.

N

Neem

Ayurvedic botanical

Neem is a tree native to the Indian subcontinent, and its leaves, bark and seeds have been used in Ayurvedic tradition for generations. It is often called a "village pharmacy" tree because of how many traditional uses it has.

In wellness supplements, neem is valued as part of a holistic approach to cleansing and balance. In ViriFlow™, it complements the other botanicals in the blend.*

Good to know: the exact formula, amounts per serving and any additional ingredients are listed on the supplement facts panel of the product. Review the panel for allergens and talk with your healthcare professional if you are unsure.

Six everyday pillars

Build a routine your body will thank you for

None of these steps needs to be perfect. Small, steady changes add up over weeks and months.

1. Hydrate with purpose

Water helps the urinary system flush and stay comfortable. Sip steadily through the day rather than drinking a large amount all at once.

  • Keep a bottle within reach.
  • Aim for pale-yellow urine as a simple guide.
  • Taper off fluids in the two hours before bed if night-time trips bother you.

2. Eat plenty of plants

Colorful fruits and vegetables, whole grains, beans, nuts and seeds supply fiber, antioxidants and minerals.

  • Add tomatoes, berries, pomegranate and leafy greens.
  • Choose fish, olive oil and lean proteins more often.
  • Limit highly processed foods and excess salt.

3. Move every day

Regular activity supports circulation, a healthy weight and better sleep, all of which matter for urinary comfort.

  • Walk briskly for 20 to 30 minutes.
  • Add strength training twice a week.
  • Break up long periods of sitting.

4. Care for your pelvic floor

The pelvic floor muscles help support the bladder. Kegel-style exercises are often recommended for both men and women.

  • Learn the correct muscles by pausing urine flow once, then never as a habit.
  • Squeeze and hold for a few seconds, then relax.
  • Ask a professional for guidance if you are unsure.

5. Watch the irritants

Some drinks can make the bladder feel more urgent for certain people.

  • Notice how caffeine affects you and move it earlier in the day.
  • Keep alcohol moderate, especially in the evening.
  • Pay attention to very spicy or acidic foods if they bother you.

6. Sleep and de-stress

Poor sleep and high stress can make daily life feel harder in many ways, including how you experience bladder urges.

  • Keep a regular bedtime.
  • Wind down with a screen-free evening routine.
  • Try breathing exercises, a short walk or stretching.

When to see a healthcare professional

A supplement is not a substitute for medical care. Make an appointment promptly if you notice:

  • Blood in your urine or pain or burning when you urinate.
  • Sudden inability to urinate, or a very weak or interrupted stream.
  • Fever, chills or pain in the back, side or pelvis.
  • A sudden change in urinary habits that worries you.

Get urgent medical help if you cannot urinate at all.
